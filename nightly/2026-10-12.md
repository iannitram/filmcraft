### PR 408 — VA-API hardware decoding + H.264/H.265 export encoding

- Builds clean, `cargo clippy --all-targets -D warnings` clean.
- **Bug (test robustness, not a GPU-dependent bug):** `crates/platform/tests/vaapi_export.rs:116` (`what_the_gpu_does_not_take_goes_to_the_software_encoder`) assumes a real VA-API hardware encoder is present and has no skip-if-absent guard, unlike its sibling test `hardware_export_matches_the_software_export` (same file, line ~94), which checks `if after.sessions == before.sessions { eprintln!("SKIPPED..."); return; }` before asserting anything. This sandbox has no `/dev/dri` (no GPU), so `filmcraft_platform::register()` never registers the VA-API encoder factory at all (`crates/platform/src/lib.rs:85-87` returns `Unavailable` early), and every assertion that expects a specific decline/frame count fails outright: confirmed `assertion left == right failed: left: (0, 0) right: (0, 2)` at `crates/platform/tests/vaapi_export.rs:129`.
