What it shows:
- VA-API takes H.264 and HEVC (8-bit and Main 10). CPU per 2160p frame drops from 197 to 4.2 ms (H.264), 113 to 3.1 ms (HEVC) and 152 to 4.3 ms (HEVC Main 10). At 1080p H.264, raw throughput is lower with hardware (236 vs 336 fps) (most likely the read-back of every picture to the CPU, not measured separately), but CPU per frame drops from 40 to 1.3 ms.
- VP9 and AV1 stay on FilmCraft's own decoders (0 hardware frames), although the driver offers VLD for VP9 profiles 0 and 2 and AV1 profile 0 (below). That is the gap hardware-parity.md already lists ("VA-API VP9 not yet"), and it now has numbers: 2160p VP9 at 62 to 67 fps and AV1 at 40 to 41 fps, both on the CPU.
- ProRes is software either way, as expected.
